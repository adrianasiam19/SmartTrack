Atlas Get-to-Know-You Question Bank
A collection of friendly, conversational questions that help Atlas learn how each student
thinks, learns, and grows — so it can recommend the university programmes that truly fit
them. Every question has four answer options (A, B, C, D), each reflecting a different but
equally valid way of thinking or working. There are no right or wrong answers.
