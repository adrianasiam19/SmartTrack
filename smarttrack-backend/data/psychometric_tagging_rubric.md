# Psychometric tagging rubric (heuristic)

## Categories (from Atlas Question Bank PDF)
20 categories: Learning Preferences, Study Habits, Problem-Solving Style, Curiosity,
Creativity, Leadership, Teamwork, Persistence, Motivation, Career Interests,
Decision Making, Communication, Time Management, Confidence, Academic Interests,
Technology Interest, Engineering Interest, Medical and Health Interest,
Environmental Interest, Research Interest.

## Trait tags
- Learning Preferences A–D → visual / auditory / reading_writing / kinesthetic
- Soft-skill categories → category trait + intensity by option (A high … D low)

## Programme affinity programmes
engineering, technology, medicine_health, business_law, arts_media,
natural_sciences, social_sciences, environment, research_academia

## Interest categories
Technology / Engineering / Medical / Environmental / Research: category-level
affinity scaled by option (A strongest interest).
Career Interests & Academic Interests: option-letter affinity maps.

Ambiguous options are listed in `data/psychometric_tag_review_queue.json` for manual review.
